# PHILOSOPHY.md

An unbroken log is the model path. A broken log is the shining path. The model path is a higher level than the shining path.

`path.log` is that log. It is the walk as walked: one line for each change, oldest at the top, newest at the bottom, never rewritten. A line that says the day failed is still the model path. A day left out, so the record can look finer than the walk, is the shining path.

The meters are written when they are taken. A change that landed outside the daily loop is still written down. Its meters are `n/a`, because a number that was not taken is not part of the walk.

The shining path is the lower level. It is the path we wish had happened: a gap, a silent day, or a step the process names and the tools do not take.
